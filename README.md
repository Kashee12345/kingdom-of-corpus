# Kingdom of Corpus

A free pixel art adventure RPG for first year Anatomy and Physiology. Explore fifteen lands, from Hearthvale Meadows to Genesis Gardens, battle with knowledge, and take on the Entropy Spire.

**Play:** https://kashee12345.github.io/kingdom-of-corpus/

## What is inside
- 15 lands, one per body system or topic, plus the final Entropy Spire
- 900 questions with explanations and about 137 labeled diagrams
- An explorer map with free travel to any land, practice mode and in game study notes
- Printable study notes for every land in the [notes](notes/) folder
- Saves in the browser; works on desktop, tablet and phone (runs in the browser, no install)

Demo tip: enter **Brooks** on the explorer map to unlock every land.
